# Privacy Policy: Fast Confluence Exporter (PDF & Markdown)

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

**We don't collect any data.** The extension has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no
tracking.

- **Everything happens in your browser.** The extension reads the Confluence pages you choose to
  export, using your existing Confluence login, and turns them into a PDF, Markdown or text file
  that is saved to your computer. Your content is never sent to us or to anyone else. Your
  Confluence display name may be printed on the PDF's cover page; it stays in that file.
- **It only talks to your Confluence.** Its requests go to the Confluence site you are exporting
  from, and they only read data (it never changes anything). Images embedded in your pages load
  from wherever they are hosted, just like when you view the page in Confluence.
- **Settings stay with you.** Your export preferences are stored in your browser's extension
  storage (synced between your devices if you use Chrome Sync). They never reach us.
- **Site access is your choice.** The extension can only access Confluence sites you allow, one
  site at a time. You can remove access at any time in the extension's options.

We don't sell, share or transfer any user data, because we don't have any.
